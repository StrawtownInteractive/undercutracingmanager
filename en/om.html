About Undercut Racing Manager

Undercut Racing Manager is a free, browser-based racing management game. You take over your own racing team, build it up from scratch and compete week by week against other managers – nothing to install, everything runs right in the browser on desktop and mobile.

How the game works

You are the team manager. You hire drivers, mechanics and engineers, negotiate their contracts, upgrade the team’s two cars component by component and set the tactics before every qualifying session and race. The events themselves run automatically on a fixed weekly schedule, so your decisions during the week decide how it goes:

  • Monday 20:00 – training race for entered drivers
  • Wednesday 20:00 – qualifying
  • Friday 20:00 – race
  • Sunday 20:00 – finances are updated

All times are Swedish time (CET/CEST); you can show them in your own time zone in the settings. A season consists of 10 races. After each season everyone in the game gets a year older, the tables are reset and teams move up and down between the divisions.

What you do as a manager

Build your squad

Hire drivers, mechanics and engineers, train their attributes, scout new talent and negotiate contracts. Every driver has a driving style of their own – aggressive, defensive or balanced.

Develop the cars

Upgrade the cars’ five components – tyres, engine, aerodynamics, gearbox and chassis. They affect qualifying and the race differently depending on the character of the track.

Set the tactics

Before every qualifying session and race you choose tactics and pit strategy. The drivers’ form on the day, the cars’ components and team morale then decide the result.

Run the finances

Keep track of the budget, wages, sponsorship deals and prize money. Buy and sell staff on the transfer market, where every team can bid on each other’s drivers and staff.

Climb the league pyramid

The game has five levels, from Division 5 up to the Undercut Masters League. Win your group to go up – and stay out of the bottom places to avoid relegation.

Compete against others

Chase championships, trophies and records season after season. Discuss with other managers in the forum and follow the series through standings, results and race reports.

Free to play

The game is completely free. It is funded by ads, which are only shown if you have accepted them in the cookie settings. Read more in our privacy policy.
