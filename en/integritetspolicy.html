Privacy policy

Who is responsible?
Undercut Racing Manager is run by Strawtown Interactive.

What data do we collect?
When you create an account, we store your email address, your chosen team name, and your saved game state (team, drivers, staff, finances, results and so on), solely to run the game. We never sell your data to third parties.

Logins and cheating
When you log in, we store your IP address, a random device ID kept in your browser, and the type of browser and device you use (e.g. “Safari on iPhone”). This is used only to detect whether the same person is playing with several accounts, can only be seen by the game’s administrator and is deleted automatically after 90 days.

Where is the data stored?
Account details and saved game state are stored with our database provider. Some settings, such as your cookie choice and selected language, are stored locally in your browser.

Cookies
See our cookie policy for which cookies we use – you choose which ones you allow under My account → Settings → Cookies.

Your rights
You can reset your saved game state at any time under My account → Settings, or reach out under My account → Contact if you want your data deleted or have other questions about how it is handled.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Undercut Racing Manager shows ads via Google AdSense. The ads are only loaded if you have allowed ads in the cookie settings.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookie policy

We use three categories of cookies/local storage:

Necessary – required for the game to work (e.g. login, saved game state and your cookie choice itself). Cannot be turned off.

Statistics/analytics – helps us understand how the game is used, so we can improve it. Only loaded if you allow it.

Personalized/relevant ads – lets us show more relevant ads. Only loaded if you allow it.

You choose which ones you allow, and can change your mind at any time via the "Cookie settings" button (shown at the bottom after your first choice) or under My account → Settings → Cookies.
